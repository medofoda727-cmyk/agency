## Learnings

- ElevenLabs output downloads need `elevenlabsusercontent.com` allowed in the cloud environment (added 2026-11-21).
- The restricted ElevenLabs connector has voice only, no music.

- Arabic fonts are not installed system-wide here: brands load their own (Alexandria, Cairo) from `assets/fonts/`.
- Use Western digits (1000, 12) in Arabic copy for this brand: the logo uses them, and mixing digit systems looked broken.
